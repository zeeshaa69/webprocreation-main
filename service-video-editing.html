FAQ

Common questions.

Do you shoot the footage too?

Our editing service works with footage you provide. For full production including shooting, contact us for a custom quote.

What's the turnaround time?

Reels and short clips: 48–72 hours. Long-form and commercial: 5–10 business days. Rush turnarounds available.

What file formats do you deliver in?

MP4 (H.264/H.265), MOV and platform-specific exports — 1080p, 4K, 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1 as needed.

How many revisions are included?

Standard: 2 revisions. Professional: 4 revisions. Enterprise: unlimited revisions.
