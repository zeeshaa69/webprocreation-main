Why It Matters

The cost of neglect.

01

Security risk

Outdated plugins and themes are the #1 cause of website hacks. Unpatched vulnerabilities compound silently until they're exploited.

02

Lost revenue

Every minute of downtime is lost traffic, leads and sales. Real-time monitoring catches issues before customers do.

03

Falling rankings

Slow, broken or insecure sites are penalised by search engines. Ongoing optimisation protects the SEO equity you've built.

04

Data loss

Without recent backups, a single failed update or attack can erase years of content. We back up daily, offsite, automatically.
