Choosing a Platform

Shopify or WooCommerce.

Fastest to Launch

Shopify

  • Hosted, secure and maintenance-light
  • Huge app ecosystem for fast feature builds
  • Best for direct-to-consumer brands scaling fast
  • Built-in checkout and fraud protection

WooCommerce

  • Full ownership of your WordPress store
  • Unlimited customisation of design & logic
  • Ideal for content-driven or complex catalogues
  • No monthly platform fee — you own the stack

FAQ

Common questions.

Shopify or WooCommerce — which is better?

Shopify is faster to launch and simpler to manage; WooCommerce offers more flexibility. We'll recommend the right fit based on your catalogue, budget and growth plans.

Can you migrate my existing store?

Yes. We handle complete migrations — products, customers, orders and SEO redirects — without losing data or rankings.

Do you set up payment gateways?

Yes. Payment gateway integration is included in all e-commerce packages — Stripe, PayPal and local gateways configured securely.

How do you optimise for conversions?

We implement proven CRO elements: clear CTAs, trust badges, fast checkout, abandoned-cart emails and product page best practices.
